Our PPC Process

Strategic campaigns engineered for maximum ROI

01

Account Audit & Strategy

Analyze current campaigns (if any), identify opportunities, and develop comprehensive PPC strategy aligned with business goals and budget.

02

Keyword Research

Identify high-intent keywords with optimal search volume and competition. Build negative keyword lists to eliminate wasted spend.

03

Audience Targeting

Define precise audience segments based on demographics, interests, behaviors, and custom audiences for maximum relevance.

04

Ad Creation

Write compelling ad copy and create eye-catching visuals that stop the scroll and drive clicks. A/B test multiple variations.

05

Landing Page Optimization

Ensure landing pages are optimized for conversions with clear messaging, strong CTAs, and fast loading speeds.

06

Campaign Launch

Set up conversion tracking, implement campaigns with proper structure, and monitor closely during initial launch phase.

07

Continuous Optimization

Daily monitoring and weekly optimization based on performance data. Adjust bids, budgets, targeting, and creative for better results.

08

Reporting & Scaling

Monthly performance reports with actionable insights. Scale winning campaigns and test new opportunities for growth.
