Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SEO

SEO is a long-term strategy. Most businesses start seeing improvements within 3-4 months, with significant results after 6-12 months. Competitive industries may take longer. The good news? Once you rank, you can stay there with proper maintenance.

No legitimate SEO agency can guarantee specific rankings—Google's algorithm is constantly evolving. However, we can guarantee we'll implement proven strategies that have ranked hundreds of websites. We focus on driving quality traffic and conversions, not vanity rankings.

SEO (organic) takes longer but provides sustainable, cost-effective traffic. PPC (paid ads) delivers immediate results but stops when you stop paying. We recommend both: PPC for quick wins while SEO builds long-term growth. The best strategy uses them together.

SEO requires ongoing work. Competitors are constantly optimizing, Google updates its algorithm, and fresh content helps maintain rankings. While initial optimization is crucial, continuous improvement and maintenance ensure you stay ahead of competition.

Absolutely! Local SEO is incredibly effective for businesses serving specific geographic areas. We optimize your Google Business Profile, build local citations, earn reviews, and target location-specific keywords to get you ranking in local search results and Google Maps.

We use only white-hat SEO techniques that comply with Google's guidelines, so penalties are extremely rare. If your site was previously penalized, we offer penalty recovery services to identify issues, remove bad links, and request reconsideration from Google.
